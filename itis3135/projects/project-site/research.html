Research

Current Projects

The Metabolic Biochemistry of a Nonphotosynthetic Diatom

Placing a new genus/species of nonphotosynthetic diatom on a phylogenetic tree, describing the species, identifying ideal salinity and temperature range, and annotating the genome with the goal of identifying mechanism of rewired metabolism after loss of photosynthesis. The species of study is the third diatom documented to have lost photosynthesis, and is found on a hawksbill sea turtle.

Previous projects

Hybrid Assembly of Cyanobacteria in the San Francisco Bay (2025)

With the combination of Illumina and Nanopore data built a metagenome assembled genome after benning search sequence database for target cyanobacteria species.

Growth Curves and Primer Design in Chemolithoautotrophic Proteobacteria (2024)

Grew chemolithoautotrophic arctic proteobacteria under varying temperature and environmental conditions to find maximal growth curves while using optical density on a plate reader. Designed sucessful primers to amplify the multiple copies of the rubisCO enzyme found in the genome.

Digestive Tract Comparison by Diet in Snakes (2023-2024)

Compared insectivores, piscovres, and mammal eating snakes through disection and measuring surface area of digestive tract. Aimed to correlate simplicity of diet to complexity of digestive tract.

Stomach Content Analysis for Bottlenose Dolphins (2023)

Disected over 30 bottlenose dolphins' (Tursiops truncatus) stomachs collected from stranded cetaceans. Quantified and identified otoliths with the aim of correlating diet composition to cause of death.
